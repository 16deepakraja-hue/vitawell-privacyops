Progression & Execution

  • Progressive overload: add weight or reps each week while staying in the target range.
  • Warm up: 5–10 min light cardio plus 2 warm-up sets before your first heavy lift.
  • Tempo: control the eccentric (2–3 sec down), explode on the concentric.
  • Deload: every 5–6 weeks, cut volume by ~40% for one week to recover.
  • Form first: never sacrifice technique to chase a number.

Warrior Principles

Nutrition

Eat ~1g protein per lb of bodyweight. Slight surplus to build, slight deficit to lean out. Whole foods first.

Sleep

7–9 hours nightly. Muscle is built in recovery, not just in the gym. Guard your sleep like training.

Consistency

The best program is the one you follow. Discipline over motivation — show up every scheduled day.

Hydration

3–4 L of water daily. Performance, recovery, and focus all suffer when you're under-hydrated.
